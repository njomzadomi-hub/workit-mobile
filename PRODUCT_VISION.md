# WORKIT Product Vision — Source of Truth

## Core idea
WORKIT is not a freelancer marketplace and not a traditional job board. It is a video-first hiring network for real work, especially on-site roles, where people show who they are, what they can do, and whether they are available to work.

Think: TikTok for hiring — but built around work proof, human fit, and real employment.

## Primary experience
The Feed is the heart of WORKIT.

Workers and professionals publish short vertical video pitches or proof-of-work clips:
- Who I am
- What I do
- Where I am
- Whether I am available
- What kind of work I want
- What I have built/done/performed

Employers scroll the feed and quickly understand personality, communication style, confidence, work quality, and fit before opening a profile.

Primary employer flow:
DISCOVER → WATCH → UNDERSTAND FIT → OPEN PROFILE → MESSAGE / INTERVIEW / INVITE / HIRE

Primary worker flow:
PITCH → SHOW WORK → GET DISCOVERED → CONNECT → GET HIRED

## Content in the feed
The feed mixes:
1. Short work pitches from people looking for employment or opportunities.
2. Proof-of-work videos showing projects, performances, completed jobs, transformations, builds, craft, teaching, service, etc.
3. Employer/job opportunity posts where useful.

The feed should feel alive and useful even when a user is not actively job hunting.

## Professional profile
Every profile is a personalized professional identity, not just a CV.

Possible profile modules:
- Video pitch
- Availability for work
- Profession / occupation
- Location
- Preferred work type
- Experience
- CV
- Portfolio
- Projects
- Qualifications
- Certifications
- Skills
- Tools / software
- Languages
- Work history
- Reviews
- Verified employment
- Media / performances / work examples

Profiles should adapt by profession. A dancer, electrician, architect, nurse, barber, and waiter should not all have identical profile priorities.

## Employer side
Employers should be able to discover people visually, not start with CV filtering.

Key actions:
- View profile
- Save candidate
- Message
- Invite
- Interview
- Hire

The goal is to reduce dependence on email chains, cover letters, and impersonal hiring workflows.

## Search and discovery
Search must support:
- Profession
- Skill
- Location
- Country
- Availability
- Experience
- Certification
- Tools / software
- Language
- Remote / on-site when relevant

Search supports the feed; it should not replace the feed as the core interaction model.

## Marketplace
Marketplace is secondary, not the primary identity of WORKIT.

It can support people who want to sell:
- Products
- Handmade goods
- Produce
- Art
- Services
- Lessons
- Bookings
- Other work-related commerce

But WORKIT should never look like Fiverr, Etsy, or a generic marketplace.

## Design doctrine
WORKIT should look like a category-defining global consumer app, not a business dashboard.

Principles:
- Video dominates
- Very little text in the feed
- Strong icons and gestures
- Full-screen vertical scrolling
- Premium cinematic visual language
- Immediate understanding without tutorials
- Big tap targets
- One dominant action at a time
- Avoid dense metadata overlays
- Avoid Fiverr-style cards
- Avoid LinkedIn-style feed layouts
- Avoid corporate dashboard aesthetics

Brand rule:
- WORK is white/light neutral
- only IT is violet/purple

Canonical bottom navigation:
Feed / Explore / Post / Market / Profile

## Feed information hierarchy
Show only what is immediately useful:
1. Person / face
2. Profession
3. Location
4. Availability
5. Work/pitch content
6. One proof signal
7. One primary action

Everything else belongs in the profile.

## Product positioning
WORKIT is a human-first hiring network where you see the person and the work before the CV.

Short positioning options:
- See the person. See the work. Hire better.
- Work starts with people.
- Show what you can do.
- Find people through real work.
- The fastest way to understand who you might hire.

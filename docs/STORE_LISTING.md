# WORKIT Store Listing Draft

## Product positioning
WORKIT is a video-first global work platform where people show real work, find jobs, hire professionals, sell services and build verified professional reputation.

## App name
WORKIT

## Subtitle / short description
Show your work. Find work. Hire talent.

## Short description
Discover real professionals through video, find jobs, hire workers, book services and build a living proof-of-work profile.

## Long description
WORKIT is the global work bazaar built around proof, not promises.

Show what you can do with video. Discover professionals doing real work. Find jobs matched to your skills. Search workers by profession, location, skills and availability. Hire, message, book and transact from one professional identity.

For professionals:
- Build a video-first proof-of-work profile
- Showcase projects, skills and results
- Find jobs and apply with your WORKIT profile
- Sell services, products and lessons
- Receive verified reviews after completed work
- Track earnings and grow with WORKIT Pro

For employers:
- Search workers by profession and skill
- See real work before contacting a candidate
- Post jobs and manage applicants through a hiring pipeline
- Message candidates directly
- Shortlist, interview, offer and hire in one place

For customers:
- Discover trusted professionals
- Book appointments and services
- Buy products and lessons
- See verified work and reviews before choosing

WORKIT brings professional identity, discovery, hiring, commerce and reputation into one video-first platform.

See the work. Meet the person. Make it happen.
